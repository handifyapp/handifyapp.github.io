What Handify can do

From the first letter to the finished notebook.

Your real handwriting

You draw each character a few times, and Handify alternates the variants – no two letters look the same. Letter pairs and whole words make the writing even more natural.

Straight into GoodNotes

As a GoodNotes notebook in which every line is real, erasable ink. Also as a PDF, as an image or just the writing on a transparent background.

Formulas, tables, lists

Fractions, roots, integrals, matrices and even graphs of functions. Handify takes answers from ChatGPT & co. with their headings and bullet points.

Edit your pages

Move and enlarge words, add to them with pen and highlighter, erase, insert text and photos – and reorder pages.

Pens and paper

Ballpoint, felt-tip, fountain pen, brush or pencil – on lined, squared or dotted paper, US Letter or A4, or on your own photo as the background.

Safely kept

Automatic backups on your device and backup files, e.g. for iCloud Drive. That way you take your handwriting to a new iPhone or iPad, too.

Your handwriting stays with you

Everything you draw and write stays on your device. Handify has no account and uploads your texts nowhere. You save backups yourself – wherever you want. Privacy policy

Help

Answers to the most common questions.

What do I need for Handify?

An iPhone with iOS 16 or later (iPhone 8 and newer) or an iPad with iPadOS 16 or later – that is, every iPad from 2017 on. The writing looks best on an iPad with Apple Pencil, because it senses pressure and makes the stroke thicker. Your finger works too, and on the iPhone anyway.

How do I set up my handwriting?

On first launch you draw each character in a box with guide lines – small letters up to the dashed line, capitals to the top, just like on school paper. Then you draw each character a second time and finally the small letters a third time.

Don’t copy, just write: the small differences make the handwriting look natural. After the first round you can already get started – you continue the other rounds later with “Keep drawing” on the home screen.

How does my handwriting look even more natural?

Open “My Handwriting”, tap “Write words” and write whole words. Handify splits them into letters and also saves letter pairs you write joined.

Under “My Handwriting” you redraw single characters or add more variants – the more variants, the more natural. In “Settings” you also set the size, stroke width, letter spacing and naturalness.

How do I get my text into GoodNotes?

Tap “Export” and choose “GoodNotes notebook”. Save the file with “Save to Files”, for example to iCloud Drive. Then open GoodNotes, tap ＋ and “Import”, and choose the file.

You get a new notebook in which every line is real GoodNotes ink: you can erase it, move it with the lasso and copy the writing into other notebooks. GoodNotes only takes in notebooks via “Import” – not via the share menu or the clipboard.

Can I put the writing on a page I already have?

Yes. Choose “Writing only” when you export. You get the writing as an image without a background and place it on any page with copy and paste.

Tip: in “Settings”, set the same line spacing as your template in GoodNotes, and the writing sits right on the lines.

How do I write formulas, tables and lists?

Handify understands the formatting that ChatGPT uses, for example: **bold**, # heading, lists with * or 1., checkboxes with - [ ], tables with | and formulas between $ … $.

Ready-made templates for fractions, roots, integrals, sums, matrices and graphs are under “Formulas & symbols” below the text field. Want Handify to write everything exactly as typed? Then turn off “Recognize formatting” in “Settings”.

A character is missing or I don’t like it. What now?

Characters you haven’t drawn yet are written with a template or left blank – the app shows you which ones are missing. Under “My Handwriting” → “Draw missing ones” you catch up on them.

Don’t like a variant? Tap the character under “My Handwriting” and redraw it.

Can I still edit the finished pages?

Yes. Tap “Edit” in the preview. There you can select, move, enlarge or delete words, draw with pen and highlighter, erase, and insert text and photos. Pages can be added, moved and deleted.

How do I back up my handwriting or move to a new device?

Open “Settings” and tap “Create backup” under “Data”. Save the file, for example to iCloud Drive. On the new iPhone or iPad, tap “Load backup” at the top during setup and choose the file. That also brings your handwriting from the iPad to the iPhone.

Handify also makes automatic backups on your device regularly. They disappear when you delete the app, though – so your own backup is worth it.

What does Handify cost?

Downloading and setting up are free, and every day you export 1,000 characters for free. For more, you watch a short ad video per 500 characters – or you take Premium, monthly or yearly: unlimited and without ads. The App Store shows the price in your currency.

Without Premium, the preview shows a watermark – your exported pages of course don’t have one. The subscription renews automatically and can be cancelled at any time in your Apple Account settings.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone or iPad. Handify has no account and sends your handwriting and your texts to no one. Only when you watch an ad video does Google process data for it – in the EU and UK only with your consent. “Settings” → “Delete all data” removes everything at once. More in the privacy policy.
