Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

In short: What you draw and write stays on your iPhone or iPad – Handify has no account and uploads your content nowhere. Ads only appear if you export more than 1,000 characters in a day and choose to watch an ad video; for that, Google processes data, in the EU and UK only with your consent. With Premium there are no ads.

1. Who is responsible

The provider named in the legal notice is responsible for the processing of personal data in the Handify app and on this website. For questions about privacy, you can reach me at handyfi@proton.me.

2. The Handify app

What the app stores

Handify stores data only locally on your device, in the app’s protected storage:

  • the letters, characters and letter pairs you draw (your handwriting),
  • the text you enter and your edits to the pages,
  • your settings and the chosen design,
  • photos you add as a background or on a page,
  • automatic backups of this data,
  • how many characters you have exported today (for the free allowance).

This data does not leave your device, and I have no access to it. The app uses no analytics services and uploads your content nowhere. It only uses an internet connection for ad videos and for the Premium subscription through Apple (see below). The processing on your device serves only to provide you with the app’s features.

Photos and camera

Only when you choose or take a photo yourself, for example as a background, does the app access that photo or – with your permission – the camera. The image is stored only inside the app on your device. You can change the camera permission at any time in the settings of your iPhone or iPad.

Exports and backups

When you export pages (as a PDF, image or GoodNotes notebook) or create a backup, a file is created that you save or share yourself using the features of iOS or iPadOS – for example in “Files”, in iCloud Drive or via AirDrop. Only you decide where the file goes. The services you use for this have their own privacy policies.

Device backups

If you use iCloud Backup or backups on your computer for your device, iOS or iPadOS backs up the app’s data as part of that device backup. This happens through Apple or your computer, not through me.

Deleting data

You delete all of the app’s data in Handify under “Settings” → “Delete all data”, or by removing the app from your device. Files you have exported or backed up yourself are not affected.

App Store and Apple

Handify is offered through Apple’s App Store. When you download it and make purchases, Apple processes data such as your Apple Account under its own responsibility; Apple’s privacy policy applies to this.

If you have agreed in the settings of your iPhone or iPad to share analytics with app developers, Apple provides me with anonymized crash reports and aggregated usage statistics that do not identify you. You can withdraw this consent at any time under “Settings” → “Privacy & Security” → “Analytics & Improvements”.

Premium subscription

You buy Premium through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; I receive no payment data. The app checks whether your subscription is active directly on your device through Apple’s StoreKit. You can manage and cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Ad videos (Google AdMob)

Without Premium, you export 1,000 characters for free every day. For more, you can watch a short ad video per 500 characters. The videos are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and for users outside the EU, EEA and Switzerland of Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA.

Only when you choose to watch a video does the app contact Google. Google then processes in particular your IP address, information about your device and the app (e.g. model, operating system, app version, language), a device-related identifier, and information about the ad and whether you watched the video. The purposes are delivering and billing the ads, detecting fraud and – only with your consent – personalized advertising. Handify does not request your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA).

Before the first video, Google asks for your consent where the law requires it, for example in the EU and the UK. The legal basis is then your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). If you don’t consent, Google only shows limited ads without personalization; the processing needed for that is based on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Where a law gives you the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information or of targeted advertising, for example in some US states, Google’s form offers that choice. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Handify under “Settings” → “About Handify” → “Change ad consent”.

Google may also transfer data to Google LLC in the USA; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More in Google’s privacy policy and in “How Google uses information”.

Children

Handify is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The app itself collects no personal data from anyone; the ad videos described above are only shown when a user chooses to watch one.

3. This website

Hosting on GitHub Pages

This website is provided through GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you open a page, GitHub processes the technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, which according to GitHub is logged and stored for security purposes. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is providing the website securely and reliably.

GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for which the EU Commission has issued an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). More in GitHub’s privacy statement.

No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking tools and loads no content from other servers, not even fonts.

4. Contact by email

When you write to me, I process your email address and the content of your message to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if it concerns the use of the app, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (my legitimate interest in answering requests). I delete the messages once they are dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

For email I use Proton Mail by Proton AG (Switzerland). The EU Commission has issued an adequacy decision for Switzerland.

5. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). You can object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR). Just send me an email. Since the app transfers no data to me, I usually only hold data from emails. Depending on where you live, you may have similar rights under local law, for example in the UK or in some US states; the same email is enough.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the one responsible for me:
